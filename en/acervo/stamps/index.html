Frequently asked questions

How do I find out what a stamp is worth?

Photograph the stamp in Acervo: the app identifies country, year and series from the photos and shows the price range of similar stamps on the used market (Premium). For rare pieces, check with an expert.

Is Acervo free?

Yes, to start: up to 5 items with 3 photos each, report and cloud, with no ads. Premium unlocks unlimited items, up to 20 photos per item and AI valuation, as a monthly or yearly subscription.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your collection lives on your phone and you can use it without a connection. When you’re back online, everything is saved to the cloud.

How does Acervo know what an item is worth?

With Premium, the app searches similar used items, with eBay as reference, and shows a low, typical and high price in your country’s currency. It’s an estimate to guide you, not an official appraisal.
