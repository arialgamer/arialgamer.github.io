What is Gol de Placa like

Drag from the ball to the corner of the goal and shoot. Or stand in goal and try to save it.

⚽

Real shots

The ball goes where you aim: top corner, low along the grass or curled around the wall. It hits the post and ripples the net.

🧤

Or be the goalkeeper

Tap the goal to dive. The keeper dives, jumps and catches the ball.

🏆

Cup

Quarterfinal, semifinal and final in penalty shootouts, with sudden death and a trophy at the end.

🧑

Build your player

Skin, hair, boots, number and name on the shirt, plus the celebration: airplane, dance, somersault…

🌦️

Weather and fans

Day, night under floodlights, rain and snow. Fans with drums, the wave and a party for every goal.

📒

Stickers and challenges

Stickers to collect and new challenges every day.
