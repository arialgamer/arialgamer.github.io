Deicida

One mortal against the gods of 11 mythologies ⚔️☀️

⚔️ Free🚫 No ads
  • 🏺11 chapters and 110 levels: from the Egypt of Ra to the Greece of Zeus, with a god as the boss of every level
  • ⚔️Twin khopesh: combos, dodges, parries and slow-motion finishers
  • ✨Powers of the gods: 3 per mythology; the other gods make them stronger
  • 🎙️Epic narration: the hero talks with allies and faces every boss before the fight

The full game is out on the App Store and Google Play. Free: the prologue and the first level of Egypt, up to defeating Sobek. The other chapters are unlocked with an in-game purchase: US$ 2.99 each, or all of them with the Deicida Pass for US$ 14.99.

11 mythologies, one per chapter: Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Aztec, Maya, Inca, Celtic, Slavic, Finnish, Roman, Norse and, last of all, Greek.

About Arial Gamer

Arial Gamer is an independent mobile game studio from Brazil. We make short, joyful games to play alone, with family or with friends, on iPhone and Android.

Every game is made from start to finish here: idea, art, code, narration and translation into 8 languages. Players never see ads and never need an account.
